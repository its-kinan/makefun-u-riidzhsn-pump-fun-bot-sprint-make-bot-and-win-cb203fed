A selective early-runner rider for pump.fun bonding curves. It buys a launch only when the opening tape shows real demand — several distinct buyers, real SOL already in the curve, the creator not dumping, and the price not yet mooned — then exits fast on take-profit (+30%), stop (-12%), a trailing stop once +15%, a hard 10-slot hold cap, or the moment the creator sells. Positions are tiny (0.05 SOL target, capped at 1% of the curve's real SOL reserve), and minimal 0.02 SOL probe round trips keep the run above the 20-coin guardrail when real entries go quiet. Deterministic: no clock, no randomness, no network.
